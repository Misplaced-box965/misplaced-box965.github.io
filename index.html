🌟 What is Bridgelet?

Bridgelet is a simple menu-bar application for macOS that creates a secure connection between ChatGPT and one folder on your computer. With 65 built-in MCP tools (Model Context Protocol), you can ask ChatGPT to work with your files directly - search, organize, analyze, and automate tasks in that folder without any complex setup.

💡 Why use Bridgelet? Privacy is paramount. Your data stays inside one folder. ChatGPT can read and write only there, not your entire system. This is perfect for personal documents, code projects, or any workspace where you want AI assistance.
